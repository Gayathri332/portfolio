How did I end up here?

I wanted to be an engineer since I was little — honestly, I don't even know who put that idea in my head, it was just always the plan. Right after 12th grade my resolve wobbled for a bit and I seriously considered doing a BCA instead. But then I got a seat in Computer & Communication Engineering at NMAM Institute of Technology, and that settled it. Four years, one BE, and a CGPA of 8.75 later, here I am.

Somewhere in there I also spent five months as a Backend Developer Intern, building JWT and OAuth-secured REST services in Node.js and Express — which is also where I picked up the habit this whole site is named after: reading the logs before I blame the code.

What I'm doing now

These days I'm working as an Automation Developer at Avery Dennison, designing workflows that take manual steps out of enterprise processes and debugging the ones that break in production. Alongside that, I'm working through Airtribe's backend engineering course and pointing my personal projects at AI — retrieval, embeddings, and LLM tool-use — because AI Engineering is the next chapter I'm writing toward.
